Class of 2027 Graduation Audit

For J.D. students admitted to Penn State Law, University Park, before Fall 2025 (the Class of 2027). Load your LionPATH PDF or pick your courses by hand, and the page checks them against the pre-reunification graduation rules using the post-merger course codes that LionPATH’s degree audit doesn’t recognize. It also shows concentration progress and what next semester could close.

Unofficial: verify with the registrar

This page was built by a student, not by Penn State or Penn State Dickinson Law, and it is not connected to LionPATH. It applies the written rules as published on the Intranet and in the Bulletin (sources are listed at the bottom), but only the Law School Registrar certifies graduation. Before you rely on anything here, confirm it with the Registrar’s Office (registrar@pennstatelaw.psu.edu) or your faculty advisor. The author accepts no responsibility for decisions made on the basis of this page, and the page may lag behind changes to the rules or the course lists.

Your record stays on this device

A PDF is read by this page inside your browser; courses you pick are kept in memory only. Nothing is uploaded, saved, or sent anywhere, and the page is technically blocked from making network connections (how to check). Closing the tab erases it.

Start with your record

Either LionPATH document works:

  • Advising transcript: Student Home Base › Academic Records › View Advising Transcript, then save it as a PDF.
  • Requirements report: Student Home Base › Degree Planning and Progress › My Academic Requirements › View Report as PDF.

PDF won’t read? Paste the text instead

Rebuild your record by hand, semester by semester. The checklist runs from 1L Fall to your final Spring: the ten first-year courses are filled in for you, and under each later term you type a code or title (e.g. LWREQ 934, Evidence, Immigration) and click the course to add it, then set its credits and grade. “C or better” without the letter grade is enough for every requirement except the two GPA checks; letter grades, including 1L grades, can be entered on each row.

Course crosswalk: every PSDL course

All current Penn State Dickinson Law courses in the Penn State Bulletin, with the graduation requirements and University Park concentrations each one counts toward, and the semesters the course chart lists it. This is the mapping from new course codes to the old requirements. “ABA 303(c) – UP” means the course is on the University Park 303(c) list for this cohort; “– DL” means it is only on the unified Dickinson Law list (see the key under the requirements table).
