Projects

yoy Sports Federation (ySF)

I designed and maintain the website and data infrastructure for a 12-team dynasty fantasy sports federation spanning the NFL, NBA, NHL, and MLB on Fantrax. The site features live standings, a custom power rankings engine, playoff brackets, and transaction logs—all built from scratch with a Python scraper pipeline and vanilla JavaScript frontend.

Chowshake

An iPhone and Android app for the eternal “where should we eat?” debate. Shake your phone and slot-machine reels for area, cuisine, price, and rating land on a real restaurant nearby that is open right now. You can lock the reels you already know, skip fast food and national chains, and spin together with friends, each of whom can veto the pick. Each result shows Google reviews, photos, and YouTube videos about the restaurant.

Try the beta at chowshake.com.

Class of 2027 Graduation Audit

An unofficial graduation audit for J.D. students admitted to Penn State Law at University Park before Fall 2025. After the law school’s reunification, the registrar’s system stopped crediting some courses toward the professional responsibility, seminar, and experiential requirements, so I built a tool that reads an advising transcript and checks it against the actual graduation rules. It runs entirely in the browser—nothing is uploaded or stored—and I wrote it to hand off to the law school’s IT office.
