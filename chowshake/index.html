Chowshake

Can’t decide where to eat? Shake your phone.

Chowshake is an iPhone app that spins slot-machine reels (area, cuisine, price and a minimum rating) and lands on a real restaurant near you that’s open right now. Lock anything you’re sure about, like Thai or $$, and spin the rest.

Questions

Why does it say “OSM” and show Style instead of Price?
Chowshake uses Google’s restaurant data within a small free daily allowance. When that runs out, it switches to OpenStreetMap, which is free but has no prices or ratings. It switches back the next day.
Why is a restaurant missing, or listed as open when it’s closed?
Hours and listings come from Google or OpenStreetMap. The result card says when hours aren’t listed. Check before you go.
What does “Skip big chains” skip?
National chains with roughly a hundred or more locations. Regional favorites still come up.
How do group spins work?
Tap the people button next to Spin and start a group. Friends join with the 4-letter code, link, or QR code. Anyone can cross off cuisines or prices, the host spins, and everyone gets one veto per spin. If nobody vetoes within 15 seconds, it’s decided.
How do I save a place to my Apple Maps or Google Maps lists?
Tap Save on a result and pick Apple Maps or Google Maps. That opens the restaurant’s page there, where saving to your lists is one tap. Apps can’t add to those lists directly. Favorites in Chowshake itself are under the filter button → Saved places.
How do I turn sounds or haptics off?
Tap the filter button at the top of the screen, then Settings.
