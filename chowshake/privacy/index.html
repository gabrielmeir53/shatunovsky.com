Chowshake Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Chowshake is an iPhone app made by Gabriel Shatunovsky. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and it doesn’t sell or share your data for advertising.

Your location

With your permission, Chowshake uses your precise location only while the app is open, to find restaurants near you. You can decline and search for a place by name instead. Chowshake itself never stores your location. To run a search, it is sent to:

  • Chowshake’s server at shatunovsky.com, which forwards the search to Google and returns the results. It doesn’t record your location. Like most websites, the server keeps standard logs (the connecting IP address, the time, and the type of request, but not your location) for up to 90 days for security. The site sits behind Cloudflare, which also handles these requests.
  • Google (Google Maps Platform), which provides restaurant details. Google’s handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap servers (the public Overpass service), used when Google’s daily allowance runs out or when you choose OpenStreetMap. Your location is rounded to about 100 meters before it’s sent.
  • Apple, to show the name of your city, search for places, and give directions in Apple Maps, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

If you choose to open directions or a website in another app (like Google Maps, Waze, or a browser), that app’s own privacy policy applies.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (sounds, haptics, filters, and search radius) are saved on your iPhone. Restaurants you skip with “Not this one” are forgotten when you close the app. Deleting Chowshake removes all of it.

Children

Chowshake isn’t directed to children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions? Use the contact page and mention Chowshake.
